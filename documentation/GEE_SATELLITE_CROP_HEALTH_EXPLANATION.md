## 1. Image Overview & Satellite Specifications
* **Data Source**: Google Earth Engine Cloud Environment (`COPERNICUS/S2_SR_HARMONIZED`).
* **Spacecraft**: Sentinel-2A / Sentinel-2B (European Space Agency).
* **Spatial Resolution**: 10-meter Ground Sample Distance (GSD) per pixel.
* **Clipped Boundary**: Geodesic Farm Polygon Geometry ($100\%\text{ clipped to user bounds}$).

## 2. Color Shade & Crop Health Visual Breakdown

### 🔴 Crimson Red Sectors (`#dc2626` / `#ef4444`) — Damaged / Stressed Crops
* **What It Represents**: Low Near-Infrared (NIR Band 8) reflection and high Red (Band 4) absorption ($\text{NDVI} < 0.25$).
* **Agronomic Diagnosis**: Indicates severe vegetative damage, plant dehydration, pest attack, or bare soil patches.
* **Action Required**: Inspect these red sectors for soil dryness, clogged irrigation lines, or root fungal damage.

---

### 🟡 Amber Yellow Sectors (`#f59e0b`) — Moderate / Developing Growth
* **What It Represents**: Intermediate vegetation density ($0.25 \le \text{NDVI} < 0.40$).
* **Agronomic Diagnosis**: Indicates crops in early vegetative transition or moderate canopy coverage.
* **Action Required**: Apply light nitrogen fertilizer to accelerate canopy growth into the healthy green stage.

---

### 🟢 Emerald Green Sectors (`#84cc16` / `#16a34a`) — Healthy & Flourishing Crops
* **What It Represents**: High Near-Infrared (NIR Band 8) leaf cell structure reflection and maximum chlorophyll absorption ($\text{NDVI} \ge 0.40$).
* **Agronomic Diagnosis**: High biomass density, optimal plant vigor, strong leaf chlorophyll, and sufficient water hydration.
* **Action Required**: Maintain existing irrigation and nutrient management schedule.

## 3. Mathematical Formula Behind This Visual Snapshot

The visual palette above is dynamically generated by Google Earth Engine using the **Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI)**:

$$\text{NDVI} = \frac{\rho_{\text{B8}} - \rho_{\text{B4}}}{\rho_{\text{B8}} + \rho_{\text{B4}}}$$

Where:
* $\rho_{\text{B8}}$ = Near-Infrared (NIR, $842\text{ nm}$) reflectance (reflected strongly by healthy plant cell structures).
* $\rho_{\text{B4}}$ = Red light ($665\text{ nm}$) reflectance (absorbed strongly by active chlorophyll).
